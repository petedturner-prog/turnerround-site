Our story

I'm Peter Turner. I've always had a heart for broken things: houses, cars and people. Something that's been written off isn't finished. With some graft, patience and a lot of grace, it can be brought back to life.

I believe that's a God-given calling. Turnerround takes tired, neglected and forgotten properties here in Yorkshire and turns them round into safe, warm, good-quality homes. Every renovation is a small story of recovery.

If you're new to property, or looking to invest alongside someone who does it properly and honestly, you're very welcome.
